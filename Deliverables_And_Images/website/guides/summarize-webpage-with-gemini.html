How to summarize a webpage with Gemini in Chrome

AI Vision's The Tab mode turns readable content in the page you are viewing into a concise brief. You choose the page, the audience, and the format; Gemini supplies a first pass you can check and refine.

Updated September 2, 2026 · AI Vision 2.5

Set up a focused page summary

Install AI Vision from the Chrome Web Store and add your Gemini API key in Settings. Then:

  1. Open the article, product page, or research source you want to understand in a supported HTTP or HTTPS tab.
  2. Open AI Vision from the toolbar, the right-click menu, or Alt + Shift + V.
  3. Open More modes and tools, choose The Tab, and select Summarize.
  4. Tell Gemini who the summary is for and how you want it structured. Use Copy or ask a follow-up when the answer arrives.

Prompt patterns that produce useful summaries

A short instruction becomes more reliable when it names the audience, length, and evidence you need. Try one of these starting points:

“Summarize this article for a busy product manager in five bullets. Separate facts, claims, and open questions.”
“Create a 120-word brief. Include the problem, proposed solution, evidence, and one limitation. Say when a detail is not stated.”
“Extract the product's price, refund policy, and three differentiators into a table. Preserve exact numbers and quote no more than one short phrase.”

Use the response-style setting—Concise, Detailed, Formal, Casual, Balanced, or Bullet-oriented—to keep your preferred format consistent across pages. A follow-up such as “What would change this conclusion?” is often more useful than asking for a longer first answer.

Check the summary before you reuse it

Gemini is a reading aid, not a source of record. Compare names, dates, prices, and quoted numbers with the page. Ask AI Vision to flag uncertainty, then open the relevant section yourself. For research, keep the page title and URL beside the copied answer so another reader can verify it.

Helpful boundary: The Tab reads supported, rendered webpage content. It cannot analyze Chrome internal pages, the Chrome Web Store, or other restricted pages, and a paywall, login wall, dynamic widget, or image-only page may leave important context unavailable.

Privacy and setup notes

The prompt and page context needed for the request go directly to Google's Gemini API using the key you provide. The key is stored locally by the extension's service worker. AI Vision has no developer-operated analytics, advertising, tracking, or proxy server; see the privacy notice for details. Model availability, pricing, and free-tier limits are controlled by Google.

Common questions

Can I summarize a page without selecting text?

Yes. Choose The Tab and use Summarize; AI Vision reads the supported page context instead of requiring a capture.

Can I ask a follow-up about the same page?

Yes. Follow-ups use the open panel's recent context until you close it or change mode. Ask for a different audience, format, or missing detail.

Why is part of my page missing?

Restricted pages, login or paywall content, lazy-loaded sections, and content inside some embedded frames may not be readable. Scroll the page into view or use Capture for a visible region.

How is this different from a generic AI chat tab?

AI Vision starts from the page already in front of you and keeps Capture as the simple first screen. The Tab and All Tabs are optional scopes, and browser actions require approval.
